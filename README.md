# COS Theta — Internal Business Operating System

An internal enterprise operating system for **Zero7 Consultancy** and **FILTR Coffee**, built strictly adhering to the clinical monochromatic blueprint design system from `DESIGN.md`, `tokens.json`, and `variables.css`.

---

## ⚡ Quick Start

To launch the complete application:

```powershell
python run_app.py
```

- **Web Application**: [http://127.0.0.1:8000](http://127.0.0.1:8000)
- **API Documentation**: [http://127.0.0.1:8000/docs](http://127.0.0.1:8000/docs)

---

## 🏢 Features & Architecture

- **Multi-Tenant Business Switcher**: Top header toggle between **All Operations**, **☕ FILTR Coffee**, and **🎯 Zero7 Consultancy**.
- **Executive Dashboard**: Real-time multi-business KPIs, live unified activity stream, and quick action bar.
- **Zero7 Leads & CRM**: Calling outreach log, pipeline stages, one-click conversion to active client, and two-way **Google Sheets** mirror.
- **Zero7 Clients & Deliverables**: Enterprise accounts, monthly retainer billing tracker, and milestone deliverables matrix.
- **FILTR Coffee POS & Orders**: Point of sale terminal with item customizations, receipts, and **automatic recipe stock depletion**.
- **FILTR Stock & Inventory**: Real-time stock tracking with `⚠️ LOW STOCK` warnings, restock adjustments, and supplier purchase orders.
- **Ledger & Cash Flow**: Categorized transactions (income & expenses) with daily cash reconciliation.
- **Tasks & To-Do Hub**: Team task management with **Google Tasks / Google To-Do** synchronization.
- **"The Guys" (Team & RBAC)**: Employee directory with custom usernames, passwords, roles, and business access controls.
- **Google Drive Central Vault**: Enterprise documents, SOPs, pitch decks, and FSSAI licenses.
- **AI Command Center (`Cmd + K`)**: Embedded intelligence assistant with real-time business telemetry analysis and custom AI curl/LLM API support.
- **Settings & Google Integrations**: Google Service Account JSON uploader, Sheet/Drive/Tasks IDs, and full database JSON export.
